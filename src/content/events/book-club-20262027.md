description: "Calgary Reggio Book Club brings educators, parents, and community
  members together to read, reflect, and talk about education grounded in
  children’s rights and democratic values. Each gathering offers space for
  thoughtful dialogue, shared questions, and classroom and community examples
  inspired by the Reggio Approach.  This year, we will begin with some of the
  fundamentals of Reggio philosophy by exploring a few classic articles,
  starting with Loris Malaguzzi's \"Your Image of the Child: Where Teaching
  Begins.\" Together, we will examine how we can ground this approach within our
  unique Calgary context, stimulating fresh insights and supporting one another
  in our daily practice."
